Mapping Radio Recombination Lines Toward the Galactic Center

Current Master's Thesis

Analyzed Radio Recombination Line (RRL) data near 3.3 GHz from the 12-meter Arecibo Radio Telescope to investigate the distribution and properties of diffuse ionized gas in the Galactic Center.
